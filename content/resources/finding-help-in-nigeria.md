## Starting points, not a directory

Mira does not yet have a verified, up-to-date directory of specific clinics, hospitals, or
helplines — building and verifying that directly is planned but not complete, and we would
rather tell you that honestly than list numbers or names we have not personally confirmed. What
follows are general categories of places people in Nigeria commonly find mental health support,
so you have somewhere to start looking.

## General places to look

- **Teaching hospitals and federal medical centres** — many have a psychiatry department or
  mental health outpatient clinic, and can usually be reached through their general outpatient
  or records department to book an appointment.
- **State hospitals and general hospitals** — some have mental health services or can refer you
  to a facility that does.
- **Primary health centres** — increasingly used as a first point of contact for mental health
  concerns in some states, with referral onward where needed.
- **Non-governmental and community organisations** working in mental health — several operate in
  Nigeria, often with lower-cost or free services; a local search or asking at a health facility
  can help you find one active in your area.
- **Tele-health and phone-based counselling services** — a growing option if travelling to a
  facility is difficult, though availability and cost vary.

## If you need help right now

If you are in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, please use the **"I need help
now"** button available throughout Mira, or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## A note on cost

Cost is a real barrier for many people. It is worth asking directly whether a facility offers a
sliding scale, free consultations, or student/community rates — many do not advertise this
openly, but will tell you if you ask.
