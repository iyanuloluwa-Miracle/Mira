## For moments when things feel like too much

These are simple techniques you can use anywhere, without any equipment, when anxiety, panic, or
overwhelming feelings spike. They will not solve an underlying difficulty on their own, but they
can help you get through a hard moment.

## Slow breathing

1. Breathe in slowly through your nose for a count of 4.
2. Hold gently for a count of 4.
3. Breathe out slowly through your mouth for a count of 6.
4. Repeat for a minute or two, or as long as feels helpful.

Breathing out for longer than you breathe in tends to help the body settle.

## 5-4-3-2-1 grounding

When your mind is racing or you feel disconnected from the present moment, naming things around
you can help bring your attention back:

- **5** things you can see
- **4** things you can hear
- **3** things you can touch
- **2** things you can smell
- **1** thing you can taste

Take your time with each one. There is no need to rush.

## Grounding your feet

Press your feet firmly into the floor. Notice the surface under them — is it hard, soft, warm,
cool? This is a quiet, discreet technique you can use even while sitting somewhere public.

## If this is not enough

These techniques are meant to help in the moment. If you are having thoughts of harming yourself,
please use the **"I need help now"** button available throughout Mira right away.
