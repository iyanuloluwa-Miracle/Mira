## Signs that something needs attention sooner rather than later

Most of what Mira covers is about ordinary, common struggles with mood and anxiety. But
sometimes things move into territory that needs faster action. It is worth reaching out for
urgent help — to a trusted person, a health facility, or an emergency service — if you notice:

- Thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life, even if they feel vague or you are not sure
  you would act on them
- Thoughts of harming someone else
- A sudden, significant change in someone's behaviour — for example, giving away possessions,
  saying goodbye in a way that feels final, or a sudden calm after a period of severe distress
- Being unable to care for basic needs — eating, sleeping, personal safety — for an extended
  period
- Use of alcohol or drugs to the point where someone's safety is at risk
- Signs of psychosis — hearing or seeing things others do not, or beliefs that feel disconnected
  from reality

## What "urgent" means here

Urgent does not always mean an ambulance. It means: do not wait and see. Reach out today — to a
trusted adult, a health facility, or, if there is any risk to someone's safety right now, an
emergency service.

## If this is about you, right now

If you are having thoughts of harming yourself, please use the **"I need help now"** button
available throughout Mira. It will take you straight to support information, with no need to
finish anything else first.

## If this is about someone else

Stay with them if it is safe to do so, help them reach a trusted adult, health facility, or
emergency service, and avoid leaving them alone if you believe they are at immediate risk. You
do not have to manage this by yourself — involve others who can help.
